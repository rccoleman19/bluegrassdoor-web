Privacy notice

This page explains, in plain words, what this website collects and why.

Anonymous usage analytics

We use PostHog to count how the site is used: pages viewed, buttons and links clicked, how far a page is scrolled, and which form fields are used. These records are anonymous. They include field names and event types only, never what you type into a form or the chat.

We do not record your session, and we do not sell any information.

Information you send us

When you send one of our quote and service requests, we receive the details you enter (such as your name, contact details, and notes) so we can answer your request.

Chat with Dory

Messages you send to Dory, our website assistant, are processed by AI providers to write the replies.

Door photos

On the repair request page you can add a photo of your door. The photo is sent to an AI service to give you a first look at what may be wrong. It is analyzed for that answer and is not stored.
